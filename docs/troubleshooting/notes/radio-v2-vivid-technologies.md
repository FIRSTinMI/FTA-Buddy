---
title: V2 robot radio (Vivid Technologies): variants, PoE switch, and VH-113 retirement
date: 2026-09-23
---

Vivid-Hosting announced the V2 radio on Chief Delphi on 2026-09-23. It is branded Vivid Technologies, sold through WestCoast Products for $214.99, and expected to ship before the end of 2026. It runs 2.4 GHz plus either 5 GHz or 6 GHz. There are two part numbers: WCP-2212 for everywhere except China, and WCP-2806 for China with 6 GHz disabled. A China-locked radio can be unlocked temporarily for an event in a country that allows 6 GHz, for example the FIRST Championship. The 3-position DIP switch from the V1 (VH-109) is gone; V2 has one switch that selects the PoE input mode, and it ships set to passive PoE so it is a drop-in replacement for a V1 on a robot. AUX1 and AUX2 output 802.3at only. The housing runs about 20 °C cooler than the V1 after 12 h, and the antennas are custom tuned. The V2 is also the new field access point: Vivid is donating enough to replace every VH-113 at official events, and the VH-113 is retired. Size is about 119.2 x 52 x 32 mm.
