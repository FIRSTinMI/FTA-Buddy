---
title: Systemcore pricing and availability, required for 2027-season FRC robots
date: 2026-09-23
url: https://community.firstinspires.org/2026-systemcore-pricing-and-availability
---

FIRST announced Systemcore pricing on 2026-09-23. The 4 GB model costs $550.00 and goes on sale on the FIRST Storefront on 2026-11-18. The 2 GB model costs $450.00 and goes on sale later this year, date not yet announced. At launch only registered and secured FRC teams can buy, one 4 GB unit per team; FIRST says the limit may be lifted once demand settles. Every FRC team gets a 4 GB Systemcore in the 2027-season Kickoff Kit, and Systemcore is required on 2027-season robots, so a roboRIO is not legal then. Later, other FIRST vendors will also sell it. FTC teams wait for general vendor availability or the Storefront switch to the 2027-28 season.
